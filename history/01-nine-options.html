¶ — 9 mono options

Built from the site's actual Source Serif 4 subsets (fontkitten glyph extraction, not a redrawn approximation). All transparent, all flip black/white with system dark mode. Brandmark-first: 128px is the primary size, 32px/16px show favicon behavior for reference.

01 — sumi

Japanese minimalism
The letterform alone, optically centered. Baseline for judging the other 8. 16px OK

02 — ma

Japanese minimalism
Glyph pushed to lower-right, one hairline rule at the baseline. Negative space is the subject — reads as intentional, not off-center, once you see the rule. brandmark only

03 — hanko

Japanese minimalism
Glyph inside a thin seal ring — reads as a personal chop. Ring thins out at 16px but the silhouette still registers. 16px OK

04 — disc

Midcentury modern
Glyph knocked out of a solid disc. Strongest, most ink-heavy silhouette in the set — coaster/seal geometry. 16px OK

05 — misregister

Midcentury modern
Two copies offset ~6% diagonally, back copy faded. Offset-print misregister — Saul Bass adjacent. Doubling reads as noise below ~32px. brandmark only

06 — frame

Scandi
Lightest weight glyph, thin rounded-square frame, generous padding. Calm and airy; frame and light strokes vanish under 32px. brandmark only

07 — outline

Scandi
The glyph contour itself stroked, unfilled — draws the counter as a second line inside the bowl. Delicate, drawing-like. Closes up to a blob under 32px. brandmark only

08 — plate

Industrial
Glyph knocked out of a heavy square plate with an inset rule border. High ink coverage, engineered feel. 16px OK

09 — stencil

Industrial
Rectangular bridge gaps cut through bowl and stem, shipping-stencil style. Gaps read cleanly even small. 16px OK
